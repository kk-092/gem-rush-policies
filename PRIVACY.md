# Gem Rush — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: October 2, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Gem Rush ("Gem Rush," "the app," "we," or "us") processes information when you use the Gem Rush game on Reddit.

Gem Rush is an independently developed application built using Reddit's Developer Platform (Devvit).

## 1. Information Gem Rush Processes

Gem Rush processes only information reasonably necessary to provide its gameplay and related features.

Depending on how you use the app, this may include:

### Reddit Account Information

When you use features that require a Reddit account, Gem Rush may receive your:

- Reddit user ID
- Reddit username/display name

This information is provided through Reddit's Devvit platform. Gem Rush does not ask you to provide your Reddit password.

### Gameplay Information

Gem Rush may process and store gameplay information such as:

- Daily Challenge scores
- Best scores
- Leaderboard rank and entries
- Current and longest streaks
- Number of completed Daily Challenges
- Last completed Daily Challenge date
- Game session identifiers
- Daily Challenge start information
- Information necessary to validate and submit a score

Practice Mode data may also be stored locally within your Reddit app/browser session where applicable.

## 2. How We Use Information

Gem Rush uses this information to:

- Run the game
- Authenticate players through Reddit
- Start and validate Daily Challenge sessions
- Save scores and personal bests
- Maintain streaks and gameplay progress
- Generate Daily Challenge leaderboards
- Display player names and scores on leaderboards
- Prevent or reduce cheating, score manipulation, and abuse
- Maintain the security and reliability of the game
- Diagnose technical problems

We do not use Reddit user data to build advertising profiles or sell targeted advertising.

## 3. Leaderboards and Public Information

Gem Rush includes leaderboard functionality.

When you participate in a Daily Challenge, information displayed on a leaderboard may include:

- Your Reddit username/display name
- Your score
- Your leaderboard rank

Other players may therefore be able to see this leaderboard information.

Your internal Reddit user ID is used by the app for identifying gameplay records but is not intentionally displayed as part of the normal leaderboard interface.

## 4. Data Storage

Gameplay data is stored using Reddit's Devvit-provided storage infrastructure, including Devvit Redis.

Gem Rush currently does not use an independent external database for storing player profiles or leaderboard data.

Certain gameplay records and temporary game sessions are configured to expire automatically when they are no longer needed for their intended gameplay purpose.

Data may also be removed or updated when necessary to comply with Reddit platform requirements, user deletion requirements, legal obligations, security requirements, or technical needs.

## 5. Payments and Reddit Gold

Gem Rush may offer an optional **Support Gem Rush** purchase using Reddit Gold.

Purchases are processed through Reddit's payment infrastructure.

Gem Rush does **not** directly collect or store your:

- Credit or debit card number
- Bank account information
- External payment account credentials

Reddit may process information relating to Reddit Gold and transactions according to Reddit's own applicable terms and privacy practices.

Gem Rush may receive information necessary to determine whether an in-app transaction was successfully completed and to fulfill or acknowledge the transaction.

## 6. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Gem Rush does not intentionally request or collect:

- Your Reddit password
- Your email address
- Your real-world address
- Your phone number
- Credit or debit card details
- Bank account details
- Government identification documents
- Precise physical location
- Health information

Please do not submit sensitive personal information through the app.

## 7. Sharing of Information

Gem Rush does not sell Reddit user data.

Gem Rush does not sell, license, or share Reddit user data with data brokers or advertising networks.

Information may be processed through Reddit and Devvit infrastructure as necessary to operate the app.

Leaderboard information described above may be visible to other Reddit users as part of the game's normal functionality.

Information may also be disclosed if required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect the security, integrity, or legal rights of the app, its users, or others, subject to applicable requirements.

## 8. Tracking and Advertising

Gem Rush does not currently use third-party advertising trackers or third-party analytics trackers to follow players across websites or services.

Gem Rush does not use Reddit user data to train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

## 9. Data Retention

Gem Rush aims to retain gameplay information only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the relevant game features, maintain gameplay records, prevent abuse, and comply with Reddit platform requirements or applicable law.

Temporary Daily Challenge sessions and certain daily gameplay records use expiration mechanisms.

Some player profile information, such as streak and gameplay-progress information, may be retained for longer periods so that progress can continue between game sessions, subject to Reddit's platform requirements and applicable deletion obligations.

## 10. Account Deletion and Data Removal

Gem Rush respects Reddit's requirements regarding deleted accounts and user data.

Where required by Reddit's Developer Platform policies, identifying information associated with a deleted Reddit account must be removed from stored app data.

If you would like to request deletion of Gem Rush gameplay data associated with your Reddit account, you may contact the developer using the contact information below.

Please provide only the information reasonably necessary to identify the relevant Reddit account. Do not send passwords, financial information, government IDs, or other sensitive information.

## 11. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect gameplay information within the capabilities provided by Reddit and Devvit.

However, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

Gem Rush does not directly store payment-card information.

## 12. Children's Privacy

Gem Rush is intended to operate within Reddit and is subject to Reddit's platform requirements.

Gem Rush does not intentionally collect information from children in violation of applicable law.

## 13. Third-Party Platform

Gem Rush operates on Reddit and relies on Reddit's Developer Platform.

Your use of Reddit itself is governed separately by Reddit's own terms and privacy practices.

Gem Rush is independently developed and, unless explicitly stated otherwise, is not an official Reddit product and is not endorsed, sponsored, or operated by Reddit.

## 14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Gem Rush's features, data practices, platform requirements, or applicable legal requirements change.

When this policy is materially updated, the effective date at the top of this document will be updated.
